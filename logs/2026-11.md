**Found:**
- **CBP is unusable after 2016.** From 2017, county cells with fewer than 3 establishments are suppressed, and county rows fall from 2,588 to 669. QCEW private establishments became the main measure instead (511110 through 2021, 513110 from 2022).
- **QCEW does not match Medill.** QCEW has 816 zero-newspaper counties in 2025; Medill reports 212. QCEW counts establishments, not news outlets.
- **Zero-newspaper counties are small.** Their mean population is 23,487, against 250,731 in counties with 2 or more papers.
- **Overlap and power:**
  - 635 of 1,066 dated campuses have a QCEW newspaper decline within ±3 years.
  - Only 61 campuses in 28 counties have a 1→0 newspaper transition, and 7 of them failed.
  - The 1→0 event study was not estimated: only 23 treated county-years have an announcement.
- **Announcements (C5: county FE + state×year FE, LPM, SE clustered by state):**
  - Newspapers per 10k (`q_pc10k`): +0.0030 (0.0011).
  - Zero-newspaper county (`q_zero`): −0.0091 (0.0043).
  - Both are robust to leave-one-state-out.
  - The sign is the opposite of the sorting hypothesis.
  - The count model (PPML) is null.
  - The warehousing placebo shows no within-county association (−0.007 and −0.011), but it reproduces the cross-sectional zero-county gap (PPML −0.31 to −0.61). That suggests the cross-section reflects rurality.
- **Failure (D):** null. `q_zero` estimates run from +0.02 to +0.03 (e.g. 0.0288, se 0.0360 with state×year FE), on N=1,049 with 87 failures.
- **Stalled at 24 months:** unstable across thresholds.
  - The X=24 per-capita estimate lost significance once grid controls were added: +0.51 (p 0.046) became +0.37 (0.33).
  - At X=36, `q_pc10k` is −1.17 (0.50) on only 88 campuses.
- **Time to construction start (Cox):** zero-newspaper counties start slower, HR 0.665 (p 0.023), with SEs not clustered.
- **Secrecy (E):**
  - Undisclosed developers run opposite to the prediction and are weak: `q_pc10k` column 3 is −0.088 (0.047).
  - 331 of 1,329 undisclosed facilities (25%) were named between February and June. Resolution rates are similar across news groups.
- **Grid rerun:** no conclusion changed, and the C5 estimates are identical.
- **Overall:** the project does not look viable on these data.

**Decisions:**
- **Main measure:** QCEW over CBP, because of the suppression.
- **Code break:** the 2022 NAICS change (internet publishers added) is a series break, left unadjusted and flagged.
- **Connecticut:** planning regions were allocated to the old counties using 2020 town populations. CBP CT for 2022–23 and the QCEW CT 2024 transitions were set to missing.
- **County assignment:** campuses go to counties by point-in-polygon on lat/lon; this agrees with Aterio's own FIPS for 93.7%.
- **Campus construction:** earliest announcement date and the earliest building's county; "failed" means every building was withdrawn or cancelled.
- **Stall threshold:** X=24 months (about the 80th percentile of the announcement-to-construction lag).
- **Quartiles:** min-rank, so all zero counties land in Q1.
- **Non-converged models:** PPML runs with |coef|>8 are flagged SUSPECT (one heterogeneity cell).
- **Distance outliers:** grid distances over 500 km (AK/HI campuses) were set to missing.
